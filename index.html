CAPSTONE PROJECT

RELAY

Bridging the communication gap between office and manufacturing employees.

Problem Statement

Workplace communication systems are often designed around office-based employees, leaving deskless and manufacturing workers without a centralized, accessible way to find information, get answers, and stay connected to their company

Through my project, I want to explore the communication gap between office-based employees and those who work without a desk, particularly within manufacturing industries and other physically demanding work environments. Office employees often have constant access to email, messaging platforms, meetings, and digital resources, while deskless employees may rely on paper notices, shared devices, and word of mouth to receive important information. This can make it difficult for employees to find answers to everyday questions about company policies, benefits, schedules, training, and other workplace resources.

The goal of this project is to create a more connected and inclusive workplace through the design of an internal communication app that serves as a centralized resource for employees. Rather than having to search through multiple platforms or figure out who to contact, employees would have one accessible place to find information, ask questions, and connect with the appropriate departments. Through research, surveys, and the development of a digital app prototype, I aim to create a consistent and user-friendly communication experience that supports employees regardless of their position or work environment
